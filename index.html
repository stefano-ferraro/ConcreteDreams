03 — Manifesto

We don't sell clothes.
We print the city
onto fabric.

Concrete Dreams started in a basement with one screen, a busted printer and a list of friends who'd actually wear the thing. Five years later the basement is bigger, the friends are louder, and nothing else has changed.

Every drop is small on purpose. We pick the fabric, we pick the run, we pick when to stop. If you got one, you got one. If you didn't — next time, move faster.
